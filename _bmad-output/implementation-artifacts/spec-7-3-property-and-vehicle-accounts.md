## Intent

**Problem:** A home or a car cannot be tracked, so net worth leaves out the household's largest assets (FR11).

**Approach:** Add a `property` and a `vehicle` account type, both assets created with an estimated value at a date and followed through the existing balance snapshots. Net worth, the sidebar and the balance history pick them up through the generic code.

## Boundaries & Constraints

**Always:**
- `ACCOUNT_TYPES.property = { classification: "asset", subtypes: ["single_family_home", "apartment", "second_home", "investment_property", "plot", "commercial"] }`, a French-relevant subset of Sure's `Property::SUBTYPES` keys, as 7.2 did for investments. Web kinds, ids equal to the keys: « Maison », « Appartement », « Résidence secondaire », « Bien locatif », « Terrain », « Local commercial ».
- `ACCOUNT_TYPES.vehicle = { classification: "asset", subtypes: [] }`, as Sure's `Vehicle` has none: subtype `null`, like a credit card. Web kind `vehicle` « Véhicule ».
- `transferKindOf`: `property` and `vehicle` inflows give `internal_move`, as Sure's `Transfer::Creator` falls through to `funds_movement` for them.
- The create dialog labels the amount « Valeur estimée » for both types, as Sure's « Valeur marchande estimée » and « Estimated vehicle value »; the date field keeps its label.
- No `details` for either type (`invalid_details`, as any non-loan). Transactions stay allowed and move the value, as on any asset.
- Migration generated by drizzle-kit, rebuilding `accounts` for the new check constraints, as `0019` did.

**Never:** no year built, area, address, make, model, year or mileage; no purchase price or trend panel; no automated valuation provider; no draft or multi-step creation; no new snapshot wording (« Solde » stays, as Sure's « Nouveau solde »); no change to balances, net worth, cash flow or `direction` code.

## Design Notes

Taken from Sure rather than asked: the subtype keys, a vehicle without subtypes, the asset classification, snapshots as the way to record a new value, and a transfer into either type as a `funds_movement` (Archant's `internal_move`). Departures: the property subtypes keep only the six that fit French housing (Sure's `townhouse`, `condominium`, `multi_family_home` and `agri_land` have no useful French counterpart beside « Appartement » and « Terrain »); the value is entered in one step, not Sure's three-step wizard; a transaction on these accounts moves the value, where Sure's non-cash calculator ignores it, because Archant has one balance rule for every asset and the epic forbids balance changes.
