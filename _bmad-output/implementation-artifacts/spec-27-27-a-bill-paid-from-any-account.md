## Intent

**Problem:** Archant requires an account on every recurring series (`recurring_transactions.account_id` is `NOT NULL`), so a bill paid from one account one month and another the next cannot be declared, where Sure's dialog offers « N'importe quel compte » and its `create_bill` takes no account (`origin/main` `275f2f994`).

**Approach:** Make the column nullable and read a series without an account as Sure does: it takes the reporting currency when declared, any account's line may pay it or count for it, and every read keeps it.

## Boundaries & Constraints

**Always:**
- Migration 0069, generated by `pnpm data generate` (a table rebuild); existing rows keep their account.
- Declare and edit dialogs: the account field's first option is Sure's blank « N'importe quel compte », selected by default for a new bill or income as Sure's `include_blank` on a new record; a candidate or a transaction still prefills its account. Labels stay « Payée depuis » / « Versé sur ».
- A new series without an account takes the reporting currency (Sure's family currency); an edit to « N'importe quel compte » keeps the series' currency, as Sure's edit form keeps it; an edit to an account keeps today's rules.
- Matching, as Sure's `matcher.rb`, `identifier.rb`: a series without an account accepts a line of any account (`domain/recurring/matcher.ts`, `domain/recurring/series.ts` `matches` and `rekey`, `identifier.ts` `claimOf`, the income-candidate exclusion, `recurringEntryIds`, `seriesOfTransaction`); the account score still counts for it.
- Duplicates: a declaration or an edit whose account is `NULL` checks `taken` with `account_id IS NULL`, as Sure's `account_less_duplicate?`, answering `RECURRING_ALREADY_EXISTS`.
- Reads keep it: `selectRecords` and `loadBills` left-join accounts, `accountId`/`accountName`/`accountType` nullable; `leftOut` ignores it; the export writes `account_id: null` as Sure's.
- Assistant outputs whose `account` is today `{ id, name }` give `null` for such a series; inputs unchanged (Story 27.13).

**Never:** no currency picker (Sure has one; Archant's dialog takes the currency from the account, kept); no change to detection's own patterns, which always come from one account; no change to the MCP inputs.

## Implementation Notes

- Migration `0069_recurring_any_account.sql` rebuilds `recurring_transactions`; libSQL's migrator runs it with foreign keys off, so rules and occurrences survive, which `packages/data/migrate.spec.ts` pins.
- `claimOf` keeps an exact account match, against the frozen line naming it: Sure's `identifier.rb` keys every claim on `identity_key`, account included (`candidate_patterns`, `identify_patterns_for`), so a bill without an account claims no detected pattern and « Ajouter une facture » may still offer it, as Sure's. Only the income-candidate exclusion takes a null account, as Sure's `claimed_income_source?`. The owner's rule, follow Sure exactly, settles it.
- `testing/app.ts:177` and `BillActions.tsx:57` needed no change: the first types a transaction's account, the second already handled a series' account it does not list.
- `accountRefOf` in `mcp/tool.ts` gives `null` for such a series; Story 27.13 reshapes the rest.
