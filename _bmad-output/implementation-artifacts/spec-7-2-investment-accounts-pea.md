## Intent

**Problem:** A PEA, an assurance-vie or a compte-titres cannot be tracked, so net worth leaves out the household's investments, and a contribution matched as a transfer becomes an `internal_move` that the monthly cash flow ignores (FR10, FR33).

**Approach:** Add an `investment` account type, an asset tracked by its value through the existing balance snapshots, with no holdings. A transfer landing on it from a non-investment account becomes `investment_contribution`; net worth and cash flow already handle the rest.

## Boundaries & Constraints

**Always:**
- `ACCOUNT_TYPES.investment = { classification: "asset", subtypes: ["pea", "assurance_vie", "brokerage", "other"] }`, keys as Sure's `Investment::SUBTYPES` (Sure has no compte-titres key; `brokerage` is its equivalent). Web kinds `pea` « PEA », `assurance_vie` « Assurance-vie », `brokerage` « Compte-titres », `other_investment` « Autre placement ».
- `ACCOUNT_SUBTYPES` stays a list of distinct values: `other` now belongs to two types.
- `transferKindOf(inflowType, outflowType)`, as Sure's `Transfer::Creator#outflow_transaction_kind`: loan inflow → `loan_payment`; credit card → `credit_card_payment`; investment inflow from a non-investment account → `investment_contribution`; anything else, including investment to investment and investment to depository, → `internal_move`. Manual and automatic matches both go through `transferBetween`, which passes both sides' types.
- An investment has no `details` (`invalid_details` as any non-loan). Its opening balance is its value, positive, as a depository's. Transactions on it stay allowed: a contribution's inflow is one.
- Migration generated by drizzle-kit, rebuilding `accounts` for the new check constraints, as `0018` did.

**Never:** no holdings, trades, securities or prices; no cash/holdings split; no automatic « Investissements » category on the contribution; no new wording for snapshots (« Solde » stays, as Sure's « Nouveau solde »); no change to `direction`, `countsInCashFlow`, `categoryCondition`, balances or net worth code.

## Design Notes

Taken from Sure rather than asked: the subtype keys (`pea`, `assurance_vie`, `brokerage`), the asset classification, snapshots as the only way to record a value, and the kind rule of `Transfer::Creator`, where a move between two investment accounts or out of one is a `funds_movement` (Archant's `internal_move`), so arbitrage inside a PEA or from a PEA to an assurance-vie is never counted as spending. Departure: « Compte-titres » is the French label of Sure's `brokerage`, as the story names it. Sure's automatic « Investment contributions » category is left out: Archant has no seeded category for it, and FR33 only asks that the outflow count as an expense.
