# Project health audit, 2026-09-30

After Epic 14 shipped as v0.2.0 and the owner ran it against a real bank, they asked what should come next and chose to check the project's health before adding features: open-source standards, security and performance, structure, and popular maintained libraries over reinvented ones. Five read-only audits ran in parallel on `main` at `82ceb39`. Nothing was modified; every measurement ran on throwaway files in `/tmp`.

Severity is the auditor's, re-read against the code. Effort: S under an hour, M half a day, L several days. The last column names the Epic 15 story that takes the finding, or why none does.

## Performance

Measured with 100,000 transactions over ten years in four accounts.
