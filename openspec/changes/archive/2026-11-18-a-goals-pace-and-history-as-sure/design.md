## Approach

Read booked, included transactions as signed account movements. Share the grouped account figures across goals and distinguish a measured zero from an unknown pace. Use reconciliation and current-anchor valuations for the balance fallback. Reuse the market movement calculation from `count-contributions-on-an-investment-backed-goal` if that change has shipped. If the pace change ships first, the investment change must extend the fallback when it adds contributions accounting. Keep the historical balances for the chart and scale them using today's share.
