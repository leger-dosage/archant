# Household preferences

## Why

Time zone, date format, country, budget month and currency are the household's settings, as Sure.

## What changes

- Ask for a country, base currency and date format during setup.
- Let the administrator change the household time zone, date format, country and budget start day.
- Use the saved base currency for cross-account totals and identify excluded accounts in other currencies.
