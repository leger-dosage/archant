# Report by tag and show biggest expenses

## Why

A household cannot use reports to follow a tagged project or see which individual payments account for its biggest expenses. Sure added both in [#5136](https://github.com/we-promise/sure/pull/5136) and [#5204](https://github.com/we-promise/sure/pull/5204).

## What changes

- Filter transaction-based report figures and output by one or more tags.
- Rank the period's largest expenses in an interactive report section and in print.

## Capabilities

### Modified capabilities

- `reports`

## Impact

The reports page and its API, section preferences, transaction CSV output and print.
