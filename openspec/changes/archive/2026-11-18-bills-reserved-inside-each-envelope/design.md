## Approach

Use outstanding occurrences after confirmed payments. Group the amounts by the bill's own budget category and count unpaid occurrences in other currencies separately. Use the budget's inclusive date range and each occurrence's stored due date, as Sure does.

The budget uses Archant's outstanding amount. Sure can also apply a spending share for installments split between a transfer and an expense. The reservation never enters the budget's allocation or spending calculations.
