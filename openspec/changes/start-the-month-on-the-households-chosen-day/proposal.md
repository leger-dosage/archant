# Start the month on the household's chosen day

## Why

A household paid on the 25th budgets from payday to payday, as Sure.

## What changes

- Calculate budget periods from the household's first day of the month, with actuals, carry-over and suggestions based on those periods. Opening an unset period SHALL generate its view without saving a budget.
