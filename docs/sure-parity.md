# Parity with Sure

What Archant does today against each functional area of [Sure](https://github.com/we-promise/sure), and why it differs where it does. Sure was read on `origin/main` at `ad0a2ba` (24 September 2026), again at `14638a701` (2 October 2026) to plan Epics 17 to 22, and last at `00dd977fb` (8 October 2026) for the audit that planned Epic 27; Sure paths below are relative to its repository.

## How to read this page

Every row has one status:

| Status                   | Meaning                                                                                                                  |
| ------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Parity                   | Archant behaves as Sure does.                                                                                            |
| Different                | Archant does it on purpose another way.                                                                                  |
| Later                    | Not built; planned to come back.                                                                                         |
| Dropped                  | Not built, and not planned.                                                                                              |
| **No decision recorded** | Archant lacks it or differs, and no planning document says so or says why. These rows are open questions, not decisions. |

Reasons cite where the decision lives:

- "Inventory" is `_bmad-output/planning-artifacts/feature-inventory.md`, the scope decisions of 2026-09-21.
- "Epics" is `_bmad-output/planning-artifacts/epics.md`, whose Epic 8 lists the rule departures.
- "AD-n" is a decision of the architecture spine, [docs/architecture.md](architecture.md).
- "Spec x.y" is the story spec `_bmad-output/implementation-artifacts/spec-x-y-*.md`; each one records its departures in its Design Notes, its "Never" list or its review triage.
- "Overview" is [project-overview.md](project-overview.md).

A "Never" item of a spec is a recorded decision to leave something out of that story. When the spec gives no reason, the row says so.

## Product-wide differences

| Topic              | Sure                                                                 | Archant                                                                                                     | Status and reason                                                                                       |
| ------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Tenancy            | `Family` groups users; one server hosts many families.               | One running instance is one household; no household entity.                                                 | Different. Inventory, product decisions: a single household is the product.                             |
